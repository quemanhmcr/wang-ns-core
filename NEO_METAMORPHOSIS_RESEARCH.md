## 2. Beltrami first unlock factors through one curl defect — EXACT / AUDIT

Let

\[
Cu_\lambda=\lambda u_\lambda,
\qquad
N(u):=P(u\times Cu)=P[X_u,C]u.
\]

Differentiating the genetic Euler field gives the exact factorization

\[
\boxed{
DN[u_\lambda]v
=P\big(u_\lambda\times(C-\lambda)v\big).
}
\]

Thus perturbations inside the same curl eigenspace satisfy

\[
Cv=\lambda v
\Longrightarrow
DN[u_\lambda]v=0.
\]

For a one-mode perturbation with `N(v)=0`,

\[
N(u_\lambda+\varepsilon v)
=\varepsilon DN[u_\lambda]v
\]

exactly.

**AUDIT.** Three perturbation classes were tested:

- same curl eigenspace: zero unlock;
- same helicity but different radius: nonzero unlock;
- same radius but opposite helicity: nonzero unlock with defect `(C-\lambda)v=-2\lambda v`.

All factorization residuals were machine zero.

**INTERPRETATION.** Radial and helicity breaking are not two new mechanisms here; both are read by the same signed-curl defect `C-\lambda`.

## 6. Odd Visibility Release Theorem — EXACT

Let

\[
u_t=N(u)-H_\nu u,
\qquad
H_\nu:=\nu C^2,
\]

and let `G` be a fixed self-adjoint reader satisfying

\[
G^*=G,
\qquad
[G,H_\nu]=0.
\]

Define

\[
W_G(t):=2\langle Gu(t),N(t)\rangle.
\]

Assume at a time `t_0`

\[
Gu=0,
\qquad
GN^{(j)}=0,
\quad 0\le j<m.
\]

Repeated differentiation of

\[
u_t=N-H_\nu u
\]

and the commutation `[G,H_\nu]=0` gives

\[
Gu^{(r)}=0,
\quad 0\le r\le m,
\qquad
Gu^{(m+1)}=GN^{(m)}.
\]

Leibniz then yields

\[
\boxed{
W_G^{(n)}(t_0)=0,
\qquad 0\le n\le2m,
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
W_G^{(2m+1)}(t_0)
=2\binom{2m+1}{m+1}
\langle N^{(m)},GN^{(m)}\rangle.
}
\]

If `G\ge0`, the first possible release coefficient is nonnegative. If `G` is an orthogonal projector, it is a positive norm square.

This is a theorem about repeated evaluation of the existing compiler, not a new visibility primitive.

### 6.1 AUDIT: orders 1, 3, 5, 7, 9

Taylor--Green support-lock experiments engineered readers annihilating successive generated shells. The first nonzero derivatives were observed at orders

\[
1,3,5,7,9
\]

with coefficients exactly matching

\[
2\binom{2m+1}{m+1}
\langle N^{(m)},GN^{(m)}\rangle.
\]

Representative values:

\[
W^{(5)}=5855.89102854,
\]

\[
W^{(7)}=1.71556169349\times10^{12},
\]

\[
W^{(9)}=2.50950078517\times10^{22}.
\]

The order-five and order-seven asymptotic Taylor ratios approached one before floating-point cancellation became dominant.

### 6.2 AUDIT: odd release requires annihilation, not mere cancellation

A counterexample was engineered with

\[
Gu=0,
\qquad
\langle N,GN\rangle=0,
\qquad
GN\neq0.
\]

Then `W=W'=0` but `W''\neq0`; moreover `W''` changed strongly with viscosity.

**DEDUCTION.** The odd parity law is an operator-annihilation result, not a consequence of a zero contraction.
