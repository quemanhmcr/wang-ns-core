## P. Team bottom line
The research programme is no longer a single pipeline that asks one ancient profile to carry every property. The literature supports a forked machine:
\[
\boxed{
\text{singularity}
\to
\text{Galilean critical activity}
\to
\begin{cases}
\text{marked singular tangent}\to\text{NEO makes the mark regular}\to\bot,\\
\text{bounded ancient tangent}+\text{record-time Galilean mark}\to\text{Local NEO}.
\end{cases}}
\]
On the bounded-ancient branch, Local NEO should classify the terminal slice, not surveil the whole orbit:
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A\to\mathcal G\to\mathcal R\to\text{backward rigidity}\to\bot.
}
\]
Here \(\mathcal A\) is a finite contact/algebraic type, \(\mathcal G\) a thick exact set geometry, and \(\mathcal R\) a backward-rigid terminal class.
The programme has now eliminated at least one genuine nontrivial terminal form: a nonzero terminal curl eigenfield is incompatible with bounded ancientness once backward uniqueness is invoked.
The two main unsolved arrows are therefore sharply separated:
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{E2}:\text{record-time bounded compactness}+\text{nonzero Galilean mark on the same sequence}}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{R1}:\text{single terminal contact}\to\text{thick exact terminal geometry}.}
\]
Everything that only generates another jet, another costume, or another sufficient criterion without moving E2/R1 should be treated as secondary.
