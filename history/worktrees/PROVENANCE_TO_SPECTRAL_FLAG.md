# Provenance to Spectral-Flag Worktree
## How a propagation programme became the whole-state spectral-signature theorem

**Historical branch:** `research/neo-provenance-propagation`
**Commit chain from the main baseline:**

- `d360c56` — add provenance and propagation programme;
- `7a89710` — establish curl spectral-flag signature;
- `55426dc` — audit spectral-flag completeness;
- `5ac3f70` — theoremize mother spectral-flag completeness.

This worktree began with a terminal-proof question and ended with a structural theorem about the whole smooth Navier--Stokes state.

That is why it is the final bridge between the worktree history and the current core.

## 1. Initial problem: defects could hide across frame, scale and time

The branch inherited small terminal defect coordinates from the C0/C1/G3 programme. Its first goal was not to invent new defects, but to track whether an existing defect survives:

- Galilean frame changes;
- singular rescaling;
- passage to a compactness limit;
- causal propagation from a past producer to a terminal contact;
- degeneration or restoration of the square anchor.

This produced provenance labels and a finite owner alphabet.

The local contact-action prism separated radial and tangential allocations between material motion and heat/square leakage.

## 2. Exact nesting showed local species could survive scale changes

On Euler-degenerate macro scales, a nested zoom at the exact `sqrt(mu)` scale restores unit square-anchor strength. The local contact classifier is unchanged by this canonical nesting.

This looked like good news for the terminal programme: perhaps Type-I and Type-II branches could share one local normal-form compiler while differing only in owner scale.

## 3. Propagation itself became another history trap

The negative result was more important.

The minimal G3 defect rates had independent Hodge/heat forcing directions. Scalar defect equations had sign-indefinite carré terms. Local Riccati dynamics could even attract geometry toward the coherent sheet.

The branch learned:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a good classifier is not necessarily a good propagation variable.}
}
\]

Trying to repair this by introducing another owner, another causal source, another rate, or another scale threatened to reproduce the same metamorphosis loop at the **proof-mechanism level**.

That was the decisive anti-history correction.

## 4. The search moved from propagation to representation invariance

The worktree then returned to the common material-curl parent discovered in the C0 branch.

Instead of asking how one chosen defect moves in time, it asked what part of the connection is visible to **every curl spectral cut**.

For each threshold `a`, define
\[
H_a=\operatorname{sgn}(C-aI).
\]
The shifted signature became
\[
\boxed{
\mathscr O_a(v)
=H_a[\nabla_v,H_a]-[\nabla_{H_av},H_a].
}
\]

At `a=0`, this reduces to the helicity-curvature slice already known from history.

But the family over all `a` is much richer than the zero fold.

## 5. Reverse compiler and layer-cake tomography

Adjoint parity gives a pointwise reverse map from the signature to the cut connection:
\[
[\nabla_v,H_a]
=H_a\operatorname{skew}\mathscr O_a(v).
\]

Then the layer-cake identity gives
\[
\boxed{
[\nabla_v,C]
=
\frac12\int_{\mathbb R}
H_a\operatorname{skew}\mathscr O_a(v)\,da.
}
\]

This was the moment the shifted family stopped looking like another obstruction family.

It was a **tomography of the mother deformation**.

## 6. Every spectral reader became a moment of the same family

For differentiable spectral readers,
\[
\boxed{
[\nabla_v,f(C)]
=
\frac12\int f'(a)
H_a\operatorname{skew}\mathscr O_a(v)\,da.
}
\]

Polynomial, exponential, trigonometric and smooth-hinge tests all passed. Higher nested commutators also reduced to the same shifted spectral flag.

Thus the whole first spectral wardrobe, and its higher jet tower, no longer supplied independent deformation species.

## 7. Exact quotient by the curl commutant

The full family detects the connection modulo operators commuting with curl:
\[
\boxed{
\mathscr O[D_1]=\mathscr O[D_2]
\iff
[D_1-D_2,C]=0.
}
\]

Initially this looked like a potential loss of real dynamics.

The branch tested that directly and found that the curl-commuting part of the physical connection is indeed nonzero. But it is unitary/isospectral motion: it preserves every quadratic curl spectral reader.

So the quotient removes **vertical spectral gauge motion**, not arbitrary physics.

## 8. Physical state faithfulness was stronger than abstract connection faithfulness

Even though an abstract connection is only known modulo the curl commutant, the physical map
\[
u\mapsto\nabla_u
\]
links horizontal and vertical pieces through the same velocity state.

Galerkin tests showed that the state-specific signature map is full rank modulo exactly the Galilean constants across increasingly large truncations.

More strongly, actual inversion recovered the state and then recovered
\[
\omega,\ S,\ S\omega,\ N(u),\ p,\ \nabla p,\ \operatorname{Hess}p,\ C^2u,\ u_t
\]
to machine precision.

## 9. Phase adversary showed the signature is not a disguised spectrum

Two states were constructed with the same complete quadratic signed-curl spectral measure, hence the same values of all tested quadratic spectral readers.

Their signatures were nevertheless separated by order one, and their Euler vector fields were also separated by order one.

Therefore the signature contains **phase/spatial compatibility information** that a spectrum alone discards.

## 10. The principal symbol closed the continuum uniqueness mechanism

The mother
\[
E_u=[\nabla_u,C]
\]
has principal symbol, on divergence-free probe directions,
\[
\boxed{
\sigma_1(E_u)(x,\xi)b
=-i\frac{\xi^TS(x)\xi}{|\xi|^2}\,\xi\times b.
}
\]

Thus the mother reads the strain quadratic form
\[
q_u(x,n)=n^TS(x)n.
\]

Six fixed directions reconstruct all five components of the trace-free symmetric strain. Incompressibility then gives
\[
\Delta u=2\operatorname{div}S.
\]

So the state is reconstructed modulo Killing fields, and uniquely on the mean-zero periodic class.

This was the decisive transition from numerical injectivity evidence to a continuum proof mechanism.

## 11. Exact norm and finite observability

The spherical identity
\[
\fint_{S^2}(n^TSn)^2\,dn=\frac{2}{15}|S|^2
\]
combined with incompressibility to produce an exact homogeneous Sobolev identity for the signature principal geometry.

Six fixed directions also gave explicit bandwidth-independent frame constants.

The inverse was therefore not merely unique; it was quantitatively stable in the smooth homogeneous setting.

## 12. The final compression: the family was larger than necessary

The shifted family was essential for discovering the geometry, especially because the critical zero fold is lower order in the UV and can be blind to principal radial/strain information.

But once the tomography and principal symbol were understood, the strongest canonical object became smaller again:
\[
\boxed{
E_u=[\nabla_u,C].
}

The full spectral flag is its canonical spectral normal form:
\[
\boxed{
E
\longleftrightarrow
\{\mathscr O_a\}_{a\in\mathbb R}.
}
\]

This is the final form of the old compiler maxim: the compiler becomes smaller as it becomes stronger.

## 13. Whole-state coordinate theorem

At the end of the branch, the theoremization commit recorded:

- reverse reconstruction from the full signature to the mother;
- principal-symbol extraction of strain;
- explicit state decoder modulo Killing symmetry;
- exact Sobolev stability;
- six-direction observability;
- a projector onto the physical signature image;
- exact conjugacy of smooth NS flow to a flow on the signature image;
- horizontal/vertical decomposition of the physical connection.

This is the direct ancestor of the current [`../../core/spectral_signature/`](../../core/spectral_signature/) directory.

## 14. What the branch did not prove

The theoremization was structural, not a regularity proof.

It did not prove:

- global smoothness of arbitrary 3D NS data;
- that nonzero signature implies blow-up;
- low-regularity completeness for every weak solution class;
- boundary-domain versions without additional functional analysis;
- that signature coordinates automatically make the singularity problem easier.

The worktree solved a different question:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Does the mother/spectral-flag geometry lose physical state information?}
}
\]

On the smooth homogeneous periodic class, the answer became **no**.
