# C0 to Material-Curl Worktree
## How a local terminal-rigidity programme discovered the common parent of many NS mechanisms

**Historical branch:** `research/neo-c0-rigidity`
**Recorded HEAD:** `2c7428e` — `research: isolate critical core curvature locking`

This worktree began with a narrow question: can a normalized C0 curl maximum be rigidified inside the bounded ancient class?

It ended somewhere very different: with the material-curl mother
\[
\boxed{
E_u=[D_u,C]
}
\]
as a common parent of pressure/Hodge, helicity, Riccati and spectral deformation, together with the first strong evidence that this geometry can reconstruct the physical NS state.

That transition is the most important prehistory of the current spectral-signature theory.

## 1. C0 killed radial stretching, not the mother

At a normalized C0 contact,
\[
\omega\cdot S\omega=0.
\]

The worktree made the geometric content explicit. Writing
\[
n=\omega/|\omega|,
\qquad
Sn=(n\cdot Sn)n+b,
\]
C0 only kills the radial coefficient. The tangent component can survive:
\[
\boxed{
Sn=b,
\qquad
b\perp n.
}
\]

In mother language,
\[
E_UU=S\omega.
\]
Thus C0 is a **radial zero set**, not a zero of the mother deformation.

This was an early sign that scalar contact conditions were only readers of a larger tensor object.

## 2. Rotating-affine anti-model destroyed the local C0 shortcut

The branch built an exact rotating affine solution with
\[
|\omega|\equiv1,
\qquad
\nabla\omega=0,
\qquad
\omega\cdot S\omega=0,
\qquad
S\omega\ne0.
\]

It solves Euler and, because the affine field is harmonic, also Navier--Stokes for every viscosity.

It is not an admissible bounded ancient blow-up profile because velocity grows linearly in space. But that is exactly why it was useful:

\[
\boxed{
\text{C0 contact algebra alone cannot imply curl-null rigidity.}
}
\]

Any valid C0 theorem had to use global admissibility, not another local identity.

## 3. The complete velocity two-jet collapsed — and still did not close C0

From `\nabla\omega=0`, the velocity Hessian becomes fully symmetric and trace-free. The worktree pushed this to a harmonic cubic normal form and tested higher angular hiding.

Again the result was negative in the right way: finite scalar/amplitude jets do not control arbitrary angular direction variation.

This reinforced the anti-loop rule inherited from the endpoint-first branch.

## 4. C1 compressed to Riccati/G3 coordinates

The branch then entered C1. The first jet could be compressed to a small algebraic alphabet involving:

- positive radial strain;
- tangential tilt;
- the Hodge/pressure scalar
  \[
  g=\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=-\Delta p;
  \]
- a Riccati/G3 coherent branch.

The minimal G3 defects became
\[
\boxed{
b=P_{n^\perp}Sn,
\qquad
\delta=6(n\cdot Sn)^2-g.}
\]

On the relevant C1 sheet,
\[
G3\iff b=0,\ \delta=0.
\]

This was a successful local compression. It was not a global closure.

## 5. Type-I supplied companions, not a final sign

The Type-I ledger gave enough large-scale decay to prove nontrivial companion geometry:

- a positive G3 core cannot fill a whole slice;
- negative-`g` companion mass must occur;
- exact G3 has zero spatial measure;
- global nilpotent forms can be eliminated;
- global nonpositive vorticity production can be eliminated;
- a normalized contact must have positive production in a finite causal region.

These were meaningful rigidity results.

But the remaining causal defect system still had multiple independent normal directions. A rank audit showed that the Hodge/heat forcing of the three G3-normal rates has full rank. There was no hidden one-dimensional sign.

## 6. Remote Hodge tuning killed another shortcut

A remote divergence-free packet can leave every prescribed local velocity jet unchanged near the observation point while changing the pressure Hessian there.

That anti-test proved:

\[
\boxed{
\text{no finite local velocity jet can own the full nonlocal Hodge response.}
}
\]

The lesson was not “pressure is a new mechanism.” It was the opposite: local normal forms were the wrong level for claiming ownership of global Hodge geometry.

## 7. The worktree asked the right new question

After C0, G3 and Hodge closure all resisted their local formulations, the branch stopped asking which terminal defect came next.

It asked:

> Are critical work, helicity torsion, pressure/Hodge action, Riccati defect, Codazzi rate and material-pair chirality independent mechanisms, or shadows of one state-dependent compatibility object?

That question produced a first candidate:
\[
\mathfrak O_u(f)=[D_u,f(C)]
\]
on the curl-generated functional calculus, with generator value
\[
\boxed{E_u=[D_u,C].}
\]

This is the direct ancestor of the later spectral-flag theory.

## 8. Why `E` looked canonical

On spectral matrix elements,
\[
[D_u,f(C)]_{xy}
=f^{[1]}(x,y)E_{u,xy}.
\]

Random-reader SVD experiments showed rank-one dependence across many first-order spectral costumes. The state amplitude lived in `E`; the reader dependence lived in the divided difference.

This was the first strong empirical statement that **many historical mechanisms were not independent species**.

The branch also found that global `E`-data can reconstruct nonlocal Hodge faces and, in finite periodic tests, reconstruct the state modulo the uniform-drift sector.

## 9. First correction: `E≠0` is not a dangerous amplitude

The `NEO_OBSTRUCTION_NS_CRUCIBLE.md` deliberately tried to destroy the hypothesis.

Harmless states such as shear, embedded 2D flow and Beltrami flow can have nonzero material-curl deformation while the nonlinear acceleration or enstrophy source vanishes.

So the branch corrected itself:

\[
\boxed{
E=[D_u,C]
\text{ is a connection/deformation carrier, not by itself “the blow-up obstruction”.}
}
\]

This correction survives in the current theory. The mother is structurally complete; nonzero mother is not a singularity criterion.

## 10. Second correction: helicity curvature is also not the final dangerous class

The worktree found dynamically active helicity/torsion curvature even on globally regular embedded 2D invariant manifolds.

Thus
\[
R_H(u)u\ne0
\]
can be real geometry without implying dangerous three-dimensional stretching.

Again, this was not a reason to discard helicity curvature. It was evidence that **a reader can be physically real and still not be the whole object**.

## 11. Connection/curvature complex

Gauss, Ricci, Bianchi and curl-Killing tests then showed that the recurring parentage behaved like a genuine projected connection/curvature complex.

The worktree temporarily named the larger object a material-curl connection/curvature complex.

This was a useful intermediate picture, but it was still too large and too history-shaped. It bundled many covariant descendants together instead of finding the smallest representation-invariant signature.

## 12. Core completeness experiments appeared before the spectral flag

Late in the branch, experiments reconstructed Navier--Stokes from a metric/Lie/curl core and stress-tested completeness across topology, boundaries and BCH-type composition.

This was the first indication that the mother geometry might be more than a terminal obstruction language: it might actually carry **the whole state**.

But the branch still lacked the final canonical representation that separated spectral cuts, identified the exact quotient by the curl commutant, and connected every spectral reader by a single tomography formula.

That was the job of the next worktree.
