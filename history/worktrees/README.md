# Research Worktree History

This directory tells the story of the isolated research worktrees that preceded the whole-state spectral-signature core.

They were deliberately isolated because each was allowed to pursue a different proof-search hypothesis without destabilizing `main`. The important history is not the number of identities produced; it is **which hypotheses failed, which state variables survived falsification, and how the ontology compressed**.

## Worktrees

### `research/neo-singularity-normal-form`

A short strategic scratch branch. It audited the former `DEFINE_PROBLEM.md` contract, separated extraction from rigidity, froze the anchor ontology, and imposed the anti-loop rule: if a calculation merely moves freedom into the next jet, stop.

Read [`SINGULARITY_NORMAL_FORM.md`](SINGULARITY_NORMAL_FORM.md).

### `research/endpoint-first-rigidity`

The largest terminal-rigidity branch. It built extraction gates, Galilean/curl microscopes, record-scale geometry, C0/C1 contact classifiers, circulation/energy cages, and a finite library of backward-rigid terminal kill classes. Its deepest lesson was negative: scalar C0 contact differentiation can remain blind to angular freedom at arbitrary finite order.

Read [`ENDPOINT_FIRST_RIGIDITY.md`](ENDPOINT_FIRST_RIGIDITY.md).

### `research/neo-c0-rigidity`

Started as a focused C0 branch and grew into the most important pre-signature laboratory. It proved the radial/angular C0 split, compressed C1 to Riccati/G3 coordinates, built Type-I companion geometry, falsified pointwise Hodge closure, and then asked whether all historical mechanisms share one material-curl parent. That produced `E=[D_u,C]`, connection/curvature crucibles, and the first completeness experiments.

Read [`C0_TO_MATERIAL_CURL.md`](C0_TO_MATERIAL_CURL.md).

### `research/neo-provenance-propagation`

Started by trying to propagate already-compiled terminal defects through time, scale and extraction topology. The propagation programme exposed another history trap: even tracking the *obstruction mechanism* through provenance layers can generate an endless new-owner hierarchy. The branch then pivoted to representation-invariant spectral cuts, discovered the shifted signature `O_a`, proved tomography back to the mother, stress-tested whole-state completeness, and theoremized the smooth periodic coordinate result.

Read [`PROVENANCE_TO_SPECTRAL_FLAG.md`](PROVENANCE_TO_SPECTRAL_FLAG.md).
