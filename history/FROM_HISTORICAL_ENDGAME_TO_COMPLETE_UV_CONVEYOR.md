The current finite-density picture suggests that many historical objects were not separate mechanisms.  They were partial readings of one possible enemy: a multi-scale critical staircase whose UV front is maintained by a finite-band, self-generated convolution motor.

The key change is not a new scalar observable.  It is the availability of a complete physical coordinate,

\[

Once this state is retained, the intended UV edge, its companion modes, side offspring, phases and polarizations are present simultaneously.  The proof no longer has to reconstruct the motor from a sequence of stock, work, traffic and differentiated-source readers.

This is the main retrospective lesson: much of the historical genealogy loop arose after an intended transfer edge was kept while the rest of the convolution network that generated it was compressed away.

## 2. X/Y and the genealogy loop

The old kinetic control-volume split between already-present stock and fresh inflow was exact at its own level.  Its weakness was architectural rather than algebraic.

A hard-shell inflow records that the shell is being fed.  It does not retain the complete same-time state that produces that inflow.  Once the side modes and phase geometry were discarded, the next natural question became “where did this inflow come from?”, followed by a new source variable, then a differentiated source, then another hidden phase or tangent direction.

The relative-translation witness made this loss concrete: identical modal stocks and cubic work data can still have different next jets.  In the present picture the interpretation is sharper:

\[
\boxed{
\text{present forward flux does not determine whether the UV motor can reproduce itself.}
}
\]

Theory 2 removes the need to promote such a quotient into the state.  The current, shell stock or traffic law may still be useful readers, while the full state remains available in the background.

## 3. Shell concentration is staircase height, not the whole enemy

The historical hard-shell theorem identified a critical shell concentration threshold.  The current finite/infinite-density split changes its interpretation.

For a dyadic shell let

\[
\mu_j=2^j\|P_j u\|_2^2.
\]

If \(\mu_j\) stays uniformly bounded, each individual stair has bounded critical height.  The critical norm can nevertheless grow through increasing logarithmic width:

\[
\|u\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}^2
\simeq \sum_j\mu_j.
\]

At the same time the kinetic stock of a stair is only

\[
\|P_j u\|_2^2\sim 2^{-j}\mu_j,
\]

so an indefinitely long bounded-height staircase is compatible with finite kinetic energy.  This is the scaling reason that repeatedly defeated attempts to pay a fixed temporal bill at every generation.

Thus the historical shell quantum is best viewed as a **height requirement** for the staircase.  It does not by itself describe the front geometry or its ability to reproduce.

The complementary branch

\[
\mu_{\rm sh}(t_n)\to\infty
\]

is a different amplitude/concentration problem.  It should not be silently identified with affine degeneration without an additional theorem.

## 5. Why the opposite-helicity sector survived every reduction

One of the most persistent historical remnants was the minority/opposite-helicity critical branch.  In the current picture that persistence is structural rather than accidental.

Write

\[
X_\pm=\|u_\pm\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}^2.
\]

The exact helical critical balance gives equal nonlinear loading of the two unsigned-helicity critical stocks, while homochiral closed-triad critical production vanishes.  Consequently a nontrivial motor that increases the critical stock cannot be purely homochiral.

Pure-helicity and Beltrami geometries therefore sit more naturally at the null/equality boundary of the final rigidity problem.  Their many vanished scalar readers were also an early warning that a reader kernel must not be confused with a physical-state kernel.

## 6. A+B was asking a reproduction question, not a budget question

The old A+B programme found strong static facts about cancellation partners, reciprocal configurations, companion outputs and null strata.  The weak point was asking these facts to pay an additive spacetime bill across infinitely many shrinking generations.

The current interpretation changes the job.

An intended forward edge uses a complete Fourier interaction.  The same active modes also participate in the rest of the quadratic convolution and can generate side outputs.  A motor that reproduces itself at the next normalized scale must therefore arrange for those side outputs to cancel, vanish by a genuine null geometry, or remain inside one closed reproducing network.

That suggests the right global upgrade of the historical A+B results:

\[
\boxed{
\text{persistent self-generated forward drift}
\ \Longrightarrow\ 
\text{unavoidable companion leakage or degeneration to a harmless null class.}
}
\]

This global network theorem is still open.  The certified A+B/reflection results are local/static ingredients for it; they are not yet the theorem itself.

The conceptual correction is important: A+B need not own a temporal wallet if its true role is to obstruct self-reproduction of one normalized complete motor.

Should a legitimate complete-core compactness theorem produce a precompact normalized trajectory, a persistent enemy would resemble a recurrent or reproducing orbit of the **complete state**, not merely of shell masses or scalar fluxes.

This is the proper setting for the current open target

\[
\boxed{
\text{No Precompact Self-Generated Critical Drift.}
}
\]

The older scalar monodromies were incomplete because reproduction depends on phases, polarizations, both helicity sheets and side offspring.  Theory 2 supplies the coordinate in which the monodromy question can finally be asked without quotienting those directions away.

Codazzi, Fisher and related projective quantities may still be useful local coordinates for how the motor changes orientation or phase.  Their historical failure was not that they were meaningless; it was the stronger demand that one of them supply a universal positive spacetime payment.
