# 08 — No-go results, hostile countermechanisms, and the remaining open theorems

## 1. Why this file exists

Theory-2 has accumulated many exact positive identities. The largest danger is turning one of them into an unjustified global sign theorem. This file records every major shortcut that has been explicitly falsified or blocked.

The purpose is anti-circularity.

## 2. Structural completeness is not regularity

The equivalences

\[
O_a(u)\ \forall a
\iff
E_u
\iff
S(u)
\iff
u
\]

and

\[
K_u\iff S(Cu)\iff u
\]

show that the Theory-2 state is complete.

They do **not** imply a coercive spacetime estimate.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{state completeness}\neq\text{Navier–Stokes regularity}.
}
\]

## 3. Vector visibility does not orient critical work

Actual Formation vectors are even under `u→-u`:

\[

\[

An odd final state reader is necessary.

The paired mixed Poisson–heat curvature has strict forward/reverse positivity,

## 5. The natural instantaneous actual-state mixed quadratic has both signs

The scalar

\[
Q_{y,\tau}(u)
=\operatorname{Re}\langle\Pi_y(u)u,\mathcal C_\tau(u)u\rangle
\]

has no universal sign.

A rank-one completed physical rectangle can cancel the low-side outputs while leaving only high-side outputs; every surviving contribution then has positive Poisson defect and negative heat defect.

Thus

\[
\boxed{Q_{y,\tau}(u)<0}
\]

for a legitimate real finite Fourier state.

Conversely isolated companion configurations can make it positive.

So this scalar cannot be the missing passivity functional.

This proves that the final theorem must be finite-step.

\boxed{

## 10. No uniform angular or companion gap in the continuum

The exact three-point angular annihilator

\[
z_h
=\delta_{-h}-2\cos h\,\delta_0+\delta_h
\]

satisfies the first-harmonic cancellation conditions for every `h`.

As

\[
h\downarrow0,
\]

Therefore no continuum theorem can claim an `O(1)` radial jump from every angular cancellation.

On the torus the lattice gives a quantized squared-frequency increment; in blow-up limits this relative increment can vanish.
