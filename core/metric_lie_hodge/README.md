# Metric–Lie / Hodge Formation Core

This directory is the canonical home of a structural formation theory for smooth incompressible Navier–Stokes.  The theory is deliberately separate from the NEO discovery methodology and from the spectral-signature core.

The primitive datum on a closed/periodic Euclidean setting is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_{NS}
=
\bigl(\mathfrak g_\sigma,[\cdot,\cdot],\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{L^2},C\bigr),
\qquad C=\operatorname{curl},
}
\]

where \(\mathfrak g_\sigma\) is the Lie algebra of divergence-free vector fields.  Equivalently, one may package the nonlinear datum as the metric Lie tensor

\[
\boxed{T(a,b,c)=\langle a,[b,c]\rangle.}
\]

The central formation form is

\[
\boxed{
\ell_{\nu,u}(a,b)
=-\langle u,[a,b]\rangle
-\nu\langle Ca,Cb\rangle.
}
\]

Let \(\mathcal L_{\nu,u}\) be its \(L^2\)-Riesz operator:

\[
\langle a,\mathcal L_{\nu,u}b\rangle=\ell_{\nu,u}(a,b).
\]

Then on the smooth divergence-free periodic class,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L_{\nu,u}=\mathcal J_u-\nu C^2,
\qquad
\mathcal J_ub=P(b\times\omega),
\qquad
\omega=Cu,
}
\]

and the Navier–Stokes equation is the diagonal flow

\[
\boxed{\partial_tu=\mathcal L_{\nu,u}u.}
\]

Thus the reversible Euler part and irreversible Stokes part are generated by one metric-Lie/curl datum rather than introduced as unrelated mechanisms.

## Claim level

The exact identities in [FORMATION_LAW.md](FORMATION_LAW.md) are standard smooth algebra/geometry once the core datum is fixed.  The stronger statement that this datum is *canonical/minimal* within broad local Euclidean model classes is supported here by explicit invariant-tensor classifications and adversarial numerical audits; it is recorded as structural evidence, not as an unrestricted classification theorem on every manifold/domain.

On domains with boundary the formal differential expression `curl` is not enough.  The domain-complete primitive is a typed oriented Hodge/de Rham realization, including boundary form/domain and harmonic cohomology; see [DOMAIN_TOPOLOGY.md](DOMAIN_TOPOLOGY.md).

This theory is not a proof of global regularity and does not assert that a single curvature scalar controls singularity formation.
