## 7. Why attraction does not mean finite-time exact capture -- DEDUCTION

The invariant in Section 6 shows an important distinction.

A trajectory with nonzero discriminant cannot reach exact G3 at a finite state under the unforced local Riccati flow.  It may nevertheless become **arbitrarily close after amplitude renormalization** as \(a\) grows.  For example in the basin \(0<\delta/a^2<9/2\), one has asymptotically

along the amplifying restricted-Euler trajectory.

Thus two statements coexist:

1. local Riccati algebra makes normalized G3 coherence dynamically attractive;
2. exact finite-state arrival on the G3 sheet from nonzero discriminant requires a normal owner outside the restricted algebra.

In full Navier--Stokes those owners are already typed:

\quad\vee\quad
