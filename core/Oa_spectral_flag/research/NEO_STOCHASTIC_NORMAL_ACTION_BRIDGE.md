# NEO Stochastic Normal-Action Bridge
## A possible probabilistic compiler between Green ownership and material defect dynamics

**Purpose.** Explain a mathematically legitimate route for joining two kinds of provenance already present in the programme:

- scalar causal ownership through a positive drift heat kernel;
- material/Riccati normal dynamics along transported fluid geometry.

The bridge is stochastic Lagrangian propagation.  This note is a **programme**, not a completed Type-I theorem.

Labels: **EXTERNAL EXACT**, **EXACT FOR SMOOTH BOUNDED COEFFICIENTS**, **PROGRAM**, **TYPE WARNING**.

## 0. Why a bridge is needed

The current Type-I causal theorem locates compulsory geometry by the scalar fundamental solution
\[
\Gamma(0,0;y,s).
\]
The minimal tangent defect, however, has a material equation whose internal Riccati part is especially simple:
\[
P D_tb+2ab=G_b^{nor},
\]
where \(G_b^{nor}\) consists only of Hodge and square-anchor terms.

Along a single deterministic material path the integrating factor for \(2a\) is obvious.  But viscosity means the terminal state is not owned by one deterministic backward path.  The scalar kernel spreads ownership over a parabolic cloud.

The correct question is therefore:
\[
\boxed{
\text{can the deterministic Riccati integrating factor be lifted to the same stochastic cloud that represents viscous transport?}
}
\]

## 1. External stochastic-Lagrangian precedent -- EXTERNAL EXACT

Constantin--Iyer give a stochastic Lagrangian representation of three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes in which viscosity is represented by Brownian perturbations of particle paths.  Equivalent backward formulations express terminal vorticity as an expectation of earlier vorticity vectors transported by stochastic deformation matrices.

This is exactly the structural pattern relevant here:
\[
\boxed{
\text{terminal vector}
=\mathbb E[
\text{random deformation propagator}
\times
\text{earlier vector}
].
}
\]

The external result is evidence and a source of legal machinery.  It does not by itself represent the nonlinear Riccati defects \((b,\delta)\) or the discriminant.

## 3. Generic matrix Feynman--Kac compiler -- EXACT FOR SMOOTH BOUNDED COEFFICIENTS

Consider a finite-dimensional defect vector \(F\) obeying
\[
\boxed{LF=M F+G,}
\]
with smooth bounded matrix coefficient \(M(x,t)\).

Let \(X_s\) be the backward diffusion ending at the terminal point and let \(R_{t,s}\) be the random matrix propagator solving the corresponding pathwise linear equation generated by \(M(X_s,s)\) with terminal identity.

It\^o's formula gives the schematic stochastic Duhamel representation
\[
\boxed{
F(x,t)
=\mathbb E\left[
R_{t,s}F(X_s,s)
+\int_s^t R_{t,\tau}G(X_\tau,\tau)\,d\tau
\right],
}
\]
with signs/orientation fixed by the chosen backward convention.

This is the probabilistic analogue of the adjoint normal-action compiler.  The matrix factor absorbs the entire bounded internal linear drift \(M\) rather than charging it as anomalous forcing.

## 4. Application template for the tangent Riccati defect -- PROGRAM

The exact tangent equation has the structure
\[
P Lb
=-2ab-PH_0n+\nu\mathcal C_b.
\]
After choosing a local tangent frame and accounting for its connection, write schematically
\[
\boxed{
Lb=M_b b+G_b^{H}+G_b^{\square},
}
\]
where the leading scalar normal attraction \(-2aI\) is part of \(M_b\).

A stochastic propagator would then yield
\[
\boxed{
 b(0,0)
=\mathbb E[
R_b\,b(s,X_s)
+\int R_b(G_b^{H}+G_b^{\square})
].
}

At a terminal G3 contact \(b(0,0)=0\).  If a causal region forces definite earlier \(b\), the formula identifies the only two ways that contribution can disappear after bounded Riccati damping:

1. stochastic directional cancellation between different paths;
2. compensating Hodge/square normal forcing.

Both are already NEO-owned phenomena.  No new angular mechanism appears.

## 8. Anti-overclaim

This note does not claim:

- that Qian--Xi's kernel alone carries the Constantin--Iyer deformation matrix;
- that the Type-I ancient class automatically satisfies every global hypothesis of the original stochastic-Lagrangian paper;
- that pathwise defect signs survive expectation;
- that a stochastic representation is itself a regularity theorem.

Its role is architectural: it supplies a principled bridge between parabolic causal provenance and material Riccati dynamics without inventing another terminal field.
