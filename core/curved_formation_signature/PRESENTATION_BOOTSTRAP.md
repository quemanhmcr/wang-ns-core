## 1. Two operator letters

Fix a generic physical state \(u_*\).  The two distinguished operators are

\[
\boxed{
C=\operatorname{curl},
\qquad
E=E_{u_*}=[\nabla_{u_*},C].
}
\]

Consider the noncommutative algebra generated by these two letters:

\[
\operatorname{Alg}(C,E)
=
\operatorname{span}\{I,C,E,C^2,CE,EC,E^2,\ldots\}.
\]

In the canonical 28-dimensional physical coordinate laboratory, curl alone generates only a small commutative spectral algebra:

\[
\dim\operatorname{Alg}(C)=6.
\]

For a generic mother snapshot, the generated algebra becomes

\[
\boxed{
\dim\operatorname{Alg}(C,E)=784=28^2.
}
\]

Thus the two-letter algebra saturates the full ambient matrix algebra.

This is not a theorem about the continuum Navier--Stokes operator algebra.  It is strong finite physical evidence of algebraic irreducibility.

## 2. Maximal word growth

The full rank is therefore not a loose Gram--Schmidt tolerance artifact.

## 5. The first word relations are spectral

The six signed curl roots in the 28-dimensional laboratory are

\[
\{\pm1,\pm\sqrt2,\pm\sqrt3\}.
\]

Hence curl satisfies

\[
\boxed{
p(C)=0,
\qquad
p(x)=(x^2-1)(x^2-2)(x^2-3).
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
p(C)
=
C^6-6C^4+11C^2-6I=0.
\]

Because

\[
E=[A,C],
\]

commuting the spectral identity with \(A\) gives the differentiated relation

\[
\boxed{
Dp_C(E)=0.
}
\]

Explicitly,

\[
\sum_{j=0}^{5}C^jEC^{5-j}
-6\sum_{j=0}^{3}C^jEC^{3-j}
+11(EC+CE)
=0.
\]

At word degree \(6\), there are \(127\) formal words but numerical rank \(125\).  The nullspace is exactly two-dimensional.

Across four generic states, the two theoretical relations \(p(C)=0\) and \(Dp_C(E)=0\) span the complete numerical nullspace, with principal cosines

\[
\boxed{1,1}
\]

to machine precision.

Thus the first relations are not arbitrary fitted dependencies.  They are the spectral law and its mother derivative.

## 6. The first non-spectral relation records physical interaction incidence

At word degree \(7\), the physical 28-dimensional algebra has

\[
255\text{ words},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}=246,
\qquad
\operatorname{nullity}=9.
\]

The two-sided ideal generated by \(p(C)\) and \(Dp_C(E)\) explains only eight relation directions.

There is exactly one additional state-independent direction.

It is linear in the mother and can be written in closed form as

\[
\boxed{
Q(C,E)
=
(C^2-I)
\big(C^2E+EC^2-5E\big)
(C^2-I)
=0.
}
\]

In spectral variables this corresponds to

\[
q(x,y)
=
(x^2-1)(y^2-1)(x^2+y^2-5).
\]

The relation vanishes on all \(26\) physically active ordered curl-sheet transitions in the base spectral window and is nonzero precisely on the four forbidden transitions

\[
-\sqrt3\leftrightarrow+\sqrt3,
\qquad
-\sqrt2\leftrightarrow+\sqrt2.
\]

The opposite \(\pm1\) transition remains allowed because the \((x^2-1)(y^2-1)\) factor vanishes there.

A pure root/multiplicity calculation proves that this extra relation does **not** follow from the curl spectrum alone.  A same-spectrum generic off-block control has only the eight spectral-ideal relations and not the ninth physical one.

So the new identity records interaction support, not merely eigenvalues.

Thus the relation is a state-independent law fingerprint in this finite category.
