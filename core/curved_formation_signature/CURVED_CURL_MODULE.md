# Curved Curl Module

## 1. Mother as covariant derivative of curl

Let \(C\) be the distinguished curl endomorphism and \(\nabla\) the formation Levi–Civita connection.  The mother is naturally an endomorphism-valued one-form:

\[
\boxed{
E=d_\nabla C,
\qquad
E_u=[\nabla_u,C].
}
\]

This identifies the mother as a first covariant deformation of curl, not as an independent physical primitive.

## 2. Curvature-corrected induced bracket

Let

\[
R(u,v)=[\nabla_u,\nabla_v]-\nabla_{[u,v]}.
\]

Jacobi of operator commutators gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
E_{[u,v]}
=[\nabla_u,E_v]-[\nabla_v,E_u]-[R(u,v),C].
}
\]

The same formula holds for every shifted cut connection

\[
A_u^a=[\nabla_u,H_a]:
\]

\[
\boxed{
A^a_{[u,v]}
=[\nabla_u,A_v^a]-[\nabla_v,A_u^a]-[R(u,v),H_a].
}
\]

A finite structural tribunal verified both identities below \(10^{-15}\).  The naive replacement

\[
E_{[u,v]}\stackrel?=[E_u,E_v]
\]

failed at order one: median normalized error \(1.252\), minimum \(1.103\).  The curvature correction had median size \(1.221\) relative to the target bracket action.  Thus the curvature term is not a perturbative decoration.

## 3. Curvature as actual curl holonomy

The correction has a direct geometric meaning.  Let \(P_h\) denote parallel transport around a corrected infinitesimal commutator loop generated by \(u,v\).  Then

\[
\frac{P_h-I}{h^2}\to R(u,v).
\]

Transport curl around the same loop:

\[
\boxed{
\frac{P_hCP_h^{-1}-C}{h^2}\to[R(u,v),C].
}
\]

For \(h=0.08,0.04,0.02,0.01,0.005\), the measured curl-holonomy errors were

\[
0.03825,\ 0.01915,\ 0.00958,\ 0.00479,\ 0.00240,
\]

with successive ratios approaching \(2\), as expected for the loop discretization after division by \(h^2\).  In the tested state,

\[
\|R\|\approx0.927,
\qquad
\|[R,C]\|\approx1.012,
\]

so the convergence is not a near-zero artifact.

## 4. Curved covariant differential

For an endomorphism-valued form, the formation connection defines the covariant exterior derivative \(d_\nabla\).  Its square is curvature action rather than zero:

\[
\boxed{d_\nabla^2=R\text{-action}.}
\]

Applied to curl,

\[
\boxed{
 d_\nabla C=E,
\qquad
 d_\nabla E=[R,C].
}
\]

The Bianchi identity gives

\[
\boxed{d_\nabla R=0.}
\]

The next tested degrees are

\[
\boxed{
 d_\nabla[R,C]=R\wedge E,
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
 d_\nabla(R\wedge E)=R\wedge[R,C].
}
\]

On full pseudospectral divergence-free fields, with low-frequency support chosen to avoid aliasing through the tested products, the residuals were

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{identity}&\text{residual}\\
\hline
\text{Jacobi of the full vector-field bracket}&5.4\times10^{-15}\\
 d_\nabla E=[R,C]&3.4\times10^{-15}\\
 d_\nabla R=0&1.3\times10^{-14}\\
 d_\nabla[R,C]=R\wedge E&4.5\times10^{-14}\\
 d_\nabla(R\wedge E)=R\wedge[R,C]&1.7\times10^{-13}
\end{array}
\]

All tested levels were nonzero, with representative norms between approximately \(0.63\) and \(0.97\).

This supports the structural tower

\[
\boxed{
C
\xrightarrow{d_\nabla}
E
\xrightarrow{d_\nabla}
[R,C]
\xrightarrow{d_\nabla}
R\wedge E
\xrightarrow{d_\nabla}\cdots.
}
\]

It is a curved covariant module, not an ordinary complex with \(d^2=0\).

## 5. Shifted spectral tomography of curvature

Because the spectral layer cake reconstructs curl from the shifted involutions \(H_a=\operatorname{sgn}(C-aI)\), commutation with curvature gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac12\int_{\mathbb R}[R,H_a]\,da=[R,C].
}
\]

A finite spectral geometry audit obtained residual \(1.72\times10^{-14}\).

More importantly, an independent full-field implementation applied \(H_a\) directly as a helical Fourier multiplier, without projecting the state into a finite Galerkin algebra.  On the chosen grid it traversed 231 signed curl roots; 40 cuts acted nontrivially on the low-frequency curvature state.  The ordinary layer cake reconstructed \(Cw\) at \(6.17\times10^{-14}\), while the curvature layer cake reconstructed \([R,C]w\) at

\[
\boxed{9.05\times10^{-14}.}
\]

The target curvature action had norm \(0.774\).

Thus the shifted spectral theory tomographs not only the degree-one mother deformation but also the tested degree-two curvature action.

## 6. Current interpretation: differential observability, not a danger tower

The historical search for a single obstruction \(O\) should not identify \(O\) with \(E\), \(K\), or any raw curvature magnitude.  Harmless 2D, Beltrami and shear controls can carry nonzero ambient mother/curvature.

The durable geometric hierarchy is instead

\[
C\quad\text{(distinguished signed spectral structure)},
\]

\[
E=d_\nabla C\quad\text{(state-complete first deformation)},
\]

\[
K=[R,C]=d_\nabla^2C\quad\text{(cross-sheet curvature sensor)},
\]

followed by higher Bianchi degrees.

The newest evidence gives this tower an inverse-geometric role.  Degree one reconstructs the cross-sheet connection; degree two generically constrains the remaining within-sheet connection through a Codazzi observability system; higher and nonlinear degrees become important on spectrally degenerate singular strata.

Thus the preferred architecture is now

\[
\boxed{
\text{state completeness}
\to
\text{spectral differential geometry}
\to
\text{generic formation-geometry reconstruction}.
}
\]

Any future regularity obstruction built from this structure is a separate theorem problem.  Core 3 itself does not identify non-flatness with blow-up danger.

## 7. Spectral reduction correction: \([R,C]\) is the cross-sheet curvature mother

The deeper campaign clarifies what the curvature mother does and does not contain.  In a curl spectral frame, split the formation connection as

\[
\nabla=V+B,
\qquad [V,C]=0,
\]

and the curvature as

\[
R=R_\parallel+R_\perp,
\qquad [R_\parallel,C]=0.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
E=[B,C],
\qquad
K=[R,C]=[R_\perp,C].
}
\]

Thus \(K\) is not “all formation curvature”.  It is the complete first functional-calculus sensor of the **cross-sheet / Codazzi part** of formation curvature.  The within-sheet Gauss/Ricci part commutes with curl and is invisible to the degree-two commutator.

Higher Bianchi degrees can nevertheless couple this vertical curvature back to visible data:

\[
d_\nabla K=R\wedge E,
\qquad
d_\nabla^2K=R\wedge K.
\]

Full physical helical tests found order-one relative contributions from the curl-commuting curvature to both degrees.  The correct interpretation is therefore a typed observability mechanism, not a claim that each higher degree is a new independent physical mechanism.
