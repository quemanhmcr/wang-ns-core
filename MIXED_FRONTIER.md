This file starts where the physical core stops.  Its job is to prevent the programme from solving a recurrence that the observer accidentally created, while isolating the smallest genuine Navier–Stokes problem still open.

- normalized critical quantities promoted to globally finite reset budgets.

The two eventually-pure recurrence routes also cannot support an infinite interior tail: consecutive high strain is finite, and consecutive **physically selected signed-good generated HH** is finite.

## 2. Three ontological layers

A useful cleanup is to stop putting all useful quantities in one “owner” enum.

### A. Kinetic-energy causality

Persistent physical modal stock, actual signed nonlinear work/boundary flux, viscous loss, and the initial surface `t=0` are the native objects in the exact energy continuity law.  After one Hahn, `dW+` is the canonical positive nonlinear recipient-work law.

### B. Physical geometry/state witnesses

High strain, pressure Hessian, SGS stress, critical shell, hard tail, resolved contact, signed-good geometry, coherent service and natural-corridor survival are real physical/derived observables.  They may decide which theorem applies, which scale is relevant, or which recurrence regime breaks.  Their existence does not automatically mint a new kinetic-energy charge.

### C. Representation/provenance

Smooth roles, probes, coherent cells, material old/new labels, selected families, checkpoints, entropy coordinates and donor sidecars describe or disintegrate an already-existing law.  They require a separate theorem before they can be promoted to physical event semantics.

Many historical difficulties came from mixing these three layers.

## 3. Role/probe transition quotient — DISTILLED EXACT DEDUCTION

The current canonical carrier architecture admits no independent `role_change`, `probe_change` or `role_probe_change` currency.

A role transition is one of:

1. **common physical transport/gauge:** `dot A+[G,A]=0`; its observer contribution cancels exactly;
2. **smooth physical relink:** after gauge quotient, `K_phys` gives antisymmetric same-event boundary flux, zero generation depth;
3. **coefficient obstruction:** a locator that must reenter the actual `Q^2` energy/work gate;
4. **hard-to-smooth registration:** performed after an actual hard event or critical shell already exists;
5. **arbitrary unbound replacement:** invalidates the previous certificate and fails closed; it does not become an owner.

A probe is only the readout `z=<Qu,psi>`.  Holding `Qu` fixed while changing `psi` can change `z` from full amplitude to zero without changing carrier energy, nonlinear work, dissipation or shell balance.  Therefore probe identity or coefficient magnitude cannot own a physical event.

This quotient removes an observer-facing escape route; it does not close Mixed recurrence.

## 4. Shell/service witness–charge quotient — DISTILLED EXACT DEDUCTION

A critical shell and a positive coherent/increment service law are real physical observables.  They are not representation artifacts.  But current master-facing routes use them as **state/witness consequences** of an already-typed physical law, not as independent charges.

Canonical pattern:

```text
already-typed source/work/dissipation law
        -> positive service or hard-shell consequence
        -> physical shell/tail state
        -> actual first stop, t=0, or no-hit corridor
```

Objective SGS/source work may force coherent service; fresh service may force a hard shell; pressure-pair or resolved-dissipation structure may force a shell directly.  These consequence maps may change units and may expose a state at a later physical time, but they do not clone the upstream charge merely because the same physics became readable in another observable.

If a critical shell completes a full no-hit natural corridor, the own-scale service lower lives on that very corridor and adds zero recursion depth.  If high-frequency service reaches the physical hard tail, the exact tail identity resolves the energy supply into inherited stock or true low→high nonlinear work.

A shell/service observation with no typed upstream law fails closed.  The word “service” is not permission to invent a source.

## 5. Hard-shell supplier/owner quotient — DISTILLED EXACT DEDUCTION

Mode-set continuity sharpens the previous section.  For a fixed hard shell,

\[
E_M(t)+D_M+\Phi_{out,M}=E_M(s)+\Phi_{in,M}.
\]

If `M E_M(t)>=mu_0`, then for every `0<theta<1`,

\[
\boxed{M E_M(s)\ge\theta\mu_0\quad\text{or}\quad M\Phi_{in,M}\ge(1-\theta)\mu_0.}
\]

Therefore distinguish two questions:

1. **Which true PDE theorem forced or located this shell and its geometry?**  Possible answers include resolved dissipation, pressure-pair structure, fresh SGS service, high-tail supply.
2. **What supplied the kinetic energy currently stored in the shell?**  Exact answer: earlier modal stock or actual nonlinear boundary inflow.

A theorem supplier may keep essential scale/geometry provenance, but it cannot remain a third owner of shell kinetic energy merely because it exposed the shell.

This is the cleanest current example of “physical object is not automatically physical owner.”

## 6. Source/SGS audit: what is already clear

The full Leray velocity equation has quadratic nonlinearity plus viscosity.  Smooth global SGS transfer is exactly the graded spectral nonlinear flux, so **SGS transfer is not a second transfer currency beside the nonlinear work law**.  Changing an admissible resolved cutoff repartitions the same `-Q B(u,u)` forcing rather than changing the underlying NS interaction.

Pressure is similarly nuanced: pressure Hessian is real and can drive strain geometry, while global divergence-free kinetic-energy work of pressure is zero.  Therefore a pressure theorem can be decisive for geometry without becoming an independent global energy source.

Differentiated SGS and pressure terms are true sources of derived resolved-strain variables, so they cannot be erased by a kinetic-energy argument alone.  What can now be exhausted exactly is their **downstream state/energy handoff**.

## 8. Required shape of a valid Mixed theorem

A satisfactory theorem must show that every infinite interior sequence of genuine physical events does at least one of the following:

1. contains a native sublineage governed by an already finite telescope;
2. forces a new native monotone/telescoping physical quantity;
3. reaches a terminal transfer/cost law already bound to actual work or a true global resource;
4. drives the required physical registration to the absorbing initial surface `t=0`.

It may not create a common scalar exchange rate between physical time, log scale, work, dissipation, service, entropy or ancestry simply to make the proof telescope.

Cross-mechanism coupling is allowed only through an exact PDE identity, a conservation law, a common physical measure or an explicitly proved implication.  Similar critical scaling is not a coupling theorem.

## 11. Research protocol for every next lemma

Before proving an estimate, write the proposed physical event in the primitive NS variables and identify its units.  Ask whether it survives a change of cutoff, packet, role, probe, material reading or checkpoint while the physical trajectory is fixed.  If not, it is representation until proved otherwise.

If a positive law is used, state where its Hahn split occurred.  Downstream decompositions must be restrictions/pushforwards of that law unless a genuinely new signed physical law has been derived.

If a recurrence step is claimed, identify what changes physically between the two event times.  A state rereading, capacity threshold, shell label, material age or theorem checkpoint is not enough.

For any alleged finite budget, exhibit the actual globally bounded resource or exact scale/time geometry.  `O(1)` per critical event does not imply finitely many events.

Finally ask the Tao/averaged-NS stress test:

> **What exact true Navier–Stokes structure makes this step true?**

Acceptable answers include Waleffe/helical coefficient geometry, exact Fourier support, modal energy continuity, incompressibility/Leray orthogonality, viscosity, skew/symmetric operator structure or another explicit NS identity.  “Scaling”, “energy cancellation” and “generic bilinear bound” alone are insufficient at the frontier.
