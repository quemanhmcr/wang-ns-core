# Mixed Genuine-Owner Frontier

This is the live research handoff. Everything in `PHYSICAL_CORE.md` should be read as already available structure. The purpose here is to state exactly what remains after the artificial branches have been removed.

## 1. What is already gone

An infinite recursive path may **not** be manufactured from any of the following.

- repeated observer checkpoints or re-hardening of the same no-hit carrier;
- a second Hahn split after coarse-graining;
- interaction cells used as persistent energy wallets;
- high→high circulation relabeled as new tail supply;
- dyadic-shell count promoted to physical progress;
- same-carrier inherited stock promoted to generation;
- intrinsic material membership rereading promoted to source;
- selected-family boundary charge `R_switch` promoted to work or recursive depth;
- smooth antisymmetric `K_phys` relink promoted to new generation;
- OO/ON/NN provenance promoted to the generator of the positive service law;
- raw `material_relink` / `new_coherent_ancestry` names admitted as owners without native PDE evidence;
- exact simultaneous causes split by an invented common-unit fractional clock;
- normalized critical quantities such as `NE` or `D_V` treated as globally finite additive reset budgets.

Two genuine but **pure** infinite tails are also excluded by native PDE geometry:

1. eventually-consecutive high-strain recurrence, by the physical `3/16` descending-scale / viscous-reservoir telescope;
2. eventually-consecutive signed-good generated-HH recurrence, by the physical parabolic registration-surface backshift to `t=0`.

These results do not prove the Mixed case. They force any hypothetical infinite interior recursion to keep leaving those pure regimes.

After the native material-service quotient, a recursive observation must resolve to an actual PDE mechanism. The live owner vocabulary is intentionally small:

## 3. The Mixed problem

Suppose a hypothetical interior recursive path is infinite.

Because a pure high-strain tail is finite, infinitely many non-high-strain breakers must occur if high strain occurs infinitely often.

Because a pure signed-good generated-HH tail is finite, infinitely many breakers outside that signed-good generated regime must occur if those events occur infinitely often.

All zero-depth representation mechanisms have already been quotiented.

Therefore the unresolved object is a path that can keep **alternating among genuine native owners** so that no single pure-epoch theorem applies forever.

Schematically,

```text
source/service
   ↘
    strain/dissipation → actual HH/high-tail → source/service → ...
   ↗                         ↓
 genuine role/probe ← physical shell/service
```

The problem is not to count labels. The problem is to show that an infinite alternation cannot evade all of the native physical laws simultaneously.

A satisfactory Mixed theorem should have the form:

> every infinite interior sequence of genuine owner events either contains a native sublineage to which an already finite telescope applies, forces a new native monotone/telescoping physical quantity, reaches a terminal transfer/cost law, or drives the required physical registration to the absorbing initial surface.

The theorem must emerge from actual NS quantities. It must not manufacture a synthetic common clock to make heterogeneous owners comparable.

## 3A. Draft theorem target: native role/probe transition causal quotient

The first Mixed slice to attack is the remaining **role/probe-change** entry in the owner vocabulary.

The working hypothesis is not that role motion is "small". It is stronger and more physical:

> a change of smooth role or analysis probe is not itself a Navier--Stokes source. It becomes causal only through the native PDE term that physically changed carrier energy/work.

A probe change therefore cannot own generation by itself. If a probe obstruction is observed, it must reenter the physical carrier-energy/work gate before an owner is named.

Registering a smooth envelope with `QP=P` or choosing the terminal hard-state probe is downstream observation of that already existing event; the registration does not create a second event.

### Candidate theorem

For every **certified** role/probe transition used by the recursion, exactly one of the following must occur:

1. **same-state / common-transport re-anchoring:** zero generation depth;
2. **residual skew `K_phys`:** conservative same-event role flux, zero generation depth;
3. **symmetric `S`:** the existing strain/dissipation owner;
4. **hard-event re-registration:** ownership remains the already existing canonical physical work/shell event;
5. **another independently certified native PDE supplier:** route to its existing source/SGS, nonlinear-work, or shell/service type.

An arbitrary `role_change`, `probe_change`, or `role_probe_change` label which has not been resolved by one of these native mechanisms is **fail-closed** and may not enter the recursive owner state.

Equivalently, the theorem aims to remove

`genuine_role_probe_change`

as an independent causal currency. The role/probe transition is a *locator* until the actual PDE mechanism is exposed, exactly as materiality became a locator in the previous quotient.

### What must be proved, not assumed

The PR must close the following obligations without introducing a common norm or synthetic clock:

- **Q2 identity preservation:** the transition must be tied to the direct Navier--Stokes `A^2` carrier-energy law, not to coefficient magnitude alone;
- **gauge cancellation:** common affine/Kelvin role motion must cancel before physical work is charged;
- **skew closure:** the same bound `K_phys` law must give antisymmetric finite role flux, so relink remains same-event provenance;
- **probe-readout lemma:** changing only the analysis probe at a fixed physical state cannot create work, stock, dissipation, supply, or a new first-stop time;
- **hard-event anchoring:** when a new hard role is registered, its owner is inherited from the already canonical signed work / shell event, with no second Hahn law and no cloned charge;
- **master ingress barrier:** unresolved role/probe labels must fail closed rather than becoming a generic recursive cause.

A proof of these statements would shrink the Mixed owner vocabulary to native mechanisms already carrying physical work, strain/dissipation, source/SGS, or shell/service structure. It would **not yet close Mixed recurrence**; it would remove one more apparent escape route before attempting heterogeneous recurrence itself.

This candidate is intentionally a quotient theorem, not a new estimate. If it succeeds, the gain is that one less human-defined object needs a recurrence theorem at all.

## 4. What a new Mixed theorem is allowed to use

A new recurrence step may use only information physically supplied by the event it resolves.

Examples:

- strain may use actual viscous/gradient structure;
- true high-tail supply may use actual low→high crossing and tail dissipation;
- signed-good generated HH may use the certified parent-scale geometry and parabolic lifetime;
- source/SGS may use its actual work/service and any independently proved shell reentry;
- a genuine role/probe change may use only the physical interface law that proves it happened.

Cross-mechanism coupling is allowed only through an exact PDE identity, conservation law, common physical measure, or explicitly proved implication. Similar notation or matching critical scaling is not enough.

In particular, there is no permission to say “each event costs one unit” unless the unit is a genuine scale-independent globally bounded physical resource, which the current critical quantities are not.

## 7. Research discipline for every next lemma

Before adding a new object or owner, ask:

1. What exact NS/PDE quantity changed?
2. Is it stock, work, flux, dissipation, geometry, provenance, or observer bookkeeping?
3. Does it remain invariant if the representation changes while the physical field is held fixed?
4. What are its native physical units?
5. Can it create generation depth, and which theorem proves that right?
6. Is the proposed progress physical, or did the proof merely change scale, packet, cutoff, family, or checkpoint?
7. If it recurs, what **native** law makes the recurrence finite or forces a different genuine owner?
8. Does the argument preserve the one canonical positive charge without re-Hahn or cloning?
9. Is a purported interface actually certified as the same physical interface being used in the estimate?
10. Does the lemma move the Mixed frontier, or merely rebuild scaffolding that the current quotient already removed?

A theorem that cannot answer these questions should not enter the core, even if its estimate is strong.
